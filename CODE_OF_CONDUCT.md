# Code of conduct

Project Aegis is built in the open by people and AI tools. Keep it a place where anyone can contribute.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or sexual content.
- Respect privacy. Don't post anyone's personal data. Don't try to identify, locate or contact maintainers or contributors who choose to stay anonymous.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or report it privately as described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
